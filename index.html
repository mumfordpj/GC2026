Good to know
☀️ Pack well
Reef-safe sunscreen is essential — reapply after swims. Late Nov is outside cyclone season but the build-up brings sudden afternoon storms.
🚗 Bonogin is well-placed
~20-25 min to Burleigh/Tallebudgera beaches, ~40 min to Tamborine Mountain. Brisbane Airport is the long leg — allow 70-100 min plus the rental shuttle.
🐊 Swimming spots
Tallebudgera Creek and Currumbin's rock pools are calmer than the open beach if the surf's messy at Burleigh.
